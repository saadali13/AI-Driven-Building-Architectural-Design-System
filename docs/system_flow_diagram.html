How "Regenerate & Refine" Handles Cut, Add, and Adjust Requests
1 Parse Request

Simultaneous Cut + Add Parser

Example: "Delete the drawing room, cut the car porch, and add a study room". Removes cut items from checklist BEFORE adding new ones.

apply_message_to_requirements()
2 Rebalance

Gemini Edits Current ArchiScript

Explicitly instructed to NEVER say "not possible": deletes cut lines, expands/shrinks adjacent rooms to keep exact plot dimensions.

build_converse_prompt()
3 Checks & Balances

Up to 4 CAD Repair Rounds

Verifies zero physical errors (walls, doors, daylight, stairs, furniture in remaining rooms). Saves new version (v2, v3) & re-renders all sheets.

converse_design_script()
